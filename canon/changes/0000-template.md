- Proposed canon version: MAJOR.MINOR.PATCH

## Public change

State the previous public understanding and the new public truth without
including private deliberation or unrevealed context.

## Continuity impact

Identify affected published records, arcs, character/location entries, assets,
and downstream consumers.

## Effective point and recovery

Identify the exact Canon snapshot in which the change takes effect, any
superseded record, correction/rollback path, downstream notification, and
reader-safe preservation of rejected alternatives or private deliberation.

## Reader communication

Explain whether the change needs a public correction note and where it will be
published.
