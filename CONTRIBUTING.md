# Contributing

Universe contributions change the public record. Accuracy and reader safety take
priority over speed.

## Before proposing a change

1. Confirm the material is already public or explicitly approved for public
   promotion by a canon editor.
2. Choose the single authoritative record affected by the change.
3. Identify published sources, related records, continuity impact, and the
   intended canon version change.
4. Use the appropriate template and avoid repeating the same fact as editable
   truth across multiple files.

Never submit hidden continuity, unpublished twists, private world-building,
editorial planning, production context, credentials, personal data, or
confidential communications. Do not reveal private repository paths, issue
titles, branch names, or other metadata that could itself spoil unreleased work.

## Canon change requirements

Every substantive change must state:

- whether it is additive, corrective, deprecating, or retconning;
- which public facts and records are affected;
- its already-public source or canon-editor promotion attestation;
- continuity and publication impact;
- the proposed semantic canon-version increment; and
- any required updates to comic metadata, assets, or downstream consumers.

Published facts are not silently rewritten. Corrections, deprecations, and
retcons require a record under `canon/changes/` that preserves what changed and
why without exposing private deliberation.

## Pull requests

A pull request is not canon until approved by CODEOWNERS and merged. Complete the
reader-safety checklist, request canon-editor review, and verify all local links
and structured metadata. Changes affecting shared schemas or identifiers must
use a released Codex contract rather than defining a local alternative.
