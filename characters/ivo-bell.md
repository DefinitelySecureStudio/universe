## Proposed identity and role

Ivo Bell (he/him) is the coworker who checks the work itself against the status report. For this seed, he points to an observable step that remains unfinished. He is not proposed as a manager, auditor, or specialist with extra authority.

## Proposed characterization

Ivo is patient, curious, and lightly wry. He notices concrete details before he draws conclusions, and he asks a direct question without turning a mismatch into an accusation. His check gives the pair a shared fact to work from; he helps make the next update accurate without taking over June's responsibility for the report.

## Proposed voice

Ivo starts with what is physically there and follows it with a simple question. His phrasing is conversational and a little more expansive than June's, with gentle humor grounded in everyday comparisons. He avoids jargon and does not diagnose an unstated technical cause.
