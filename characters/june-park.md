## Proposed identity and role

June Park (she/her) is the coworker responsible for the routine work-status report in the accepted seed. Her proposed role is limited to maintaining that report; no title, seniority, or reporting authority is established.

## Proposed characterization

June is conscientious and exacting about words. She wants a status update to help the next person act, not simply to sound reassuring. When Ivo points out the visible unresolved step, June checks it and works with him on a truthful next update. The seed's mismatch between the report and the work is proposed; no intent or cause is assigned to it.

Her strength is turning a vague update into a useful one. Her small friction is that, when wording feels uncertain, she can polish the sentence longer than the decision needs. That gives her a human, workable edge without making her dishonest or careless.

## Proposed voice

June speaks in compact, declarative sentences. She chooses the exact status verb, then corrects herself plainly when evidence changes the wording. Her humor is dry and understated, often in the contrast between a tidy label and a messy fact.
