## Proposed relationship

June Park and Ivo Bell are coworkers at Definitely Secure. June owns the routine status report; Ivo checks what can be observed in the work. When the report says **Complete** but a step is still visibly unresolved, they compare the report with the work and coordinate a truthful next update.

Their working rhythm is complementary: June brings exact language and responsibility for the update, while Ivo brings patient attention to the concrete evidence. He can raise a mismatch without accusing her; she can correct the report without treating his observation as a challenge. That ease is a proposed coworker dynamic, not evidence of prior friendship or shared history.
